Ramon's Soundboard as a phone app

ONE-TIME SETUP (free, about 10 minutes, GitHub Pages)
1. Make a free account at github.com.
2. Tap "+" > "New repository". Name it: soundboard. Set it to Public. Create.
3. Tap "uploading an existing file" (or Add file > Upload files) and upload ALL files
   from this zip: index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest and the 4 .png icons. Commit.
4. Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: main, folder: / (root). Save.
5. After a minute your app is at:  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/soundboard/

INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE
- iPhone: open the link in Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen.
- Android: open the link in Chrome > menu > Install app (or Add to Home screen).
It opens full screen with its own icon, works offline, and the microphone works.

UPDATING TO A NEW VERSION
1. Get the new zip from Claude.
2. In your GitHub repo: Add file > Upload files > upload the new files (they replace the old ones) > Commit.
3. Open the app on your phone: an "Update" button appears at the bottom. Tap it.
Your buttons and uploaded sounds stay on the phone; updates don't erase them.
